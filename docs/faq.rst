FAQ
====

Why was SuryaPy created?
-------------------------

SuryaPy was developed during the Astro Lab Summer Internship 2024 as a lightweight toolkit for analysing solar images and detecting sunspots.

Does SuryaPy support FITS files?
--------------------------------

Currently the package mainly focuses on image-based workflows, though FITS support may be added in future releases.

Is the project still under development?
---------------------------------------

Yes. SuryaPy is still evolving and new features may continue to be added.

What libraries does SuryaPy use?
--------------------------------

The package primarily uses:

- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- AstroLab

Can I contribute?
-----------------

Yes. Contributions, suggestions, and bug reports are welcome through GitHub.
